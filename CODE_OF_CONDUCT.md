# PELLAIRI PEDS Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The PELLAIRI Pre-Execution Decision Sandbox community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone who participates. This policy applies to project-managed spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and project events.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing people, ideas, and technical decisions.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give feedback about the work and its impact, without personal attacks.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop.
- Help keep project spaces useful, accessible, and free of harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct without consent, doxing, repeated unwanted contact, deliberate disruption, and advocacy for these behaviors are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private or identifying information without permission.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, [@unrestrictedcauser-ux](https://github.com/unrestrictedcauser-ux). Include the project space, approximate date, people involved if known, and any relevant public links. Do not post sensitive details in public issues or discussions. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where practical, and share information only with people who need it to respond. Reports will be handled fairly and consistently. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or remove access for severe or repeated violations. Maintainers will explain the action and provide a way to request reconsideration. Retaliation against reporters or participants in a review is prohibited.

## Questions and appeals

Contact [@unrestrictedcauser-ux](https://github.com/unrestrictedcauser-ux) privately with questions or to appeal a moderation decision. Appeals should explain the decision being challenged and any relevant context.
